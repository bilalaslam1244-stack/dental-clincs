Questions, answered

Can I walk in, or should I reserve?

Walk-ins are welcome, but reserving on WhatsApp means you won't wait. In pain? Message us and we'll see you as early as we can.

Do you see children?

From their very first tooth. Weekend and after-school visits work best, so nobody is rushed.

I'm visiting from Singapore. Anything to know?

Fees are in Ringgit and we accept cards. Reserve with your Singapore WhatsApp number. Saturday mornings fill first.

How much will my treatment cost?

See the fee ranges above. Your dentist confirms the fee after examining you, before any treatment begins.

Are you open on public holidays?

On most, with shorter hours. Message us on the day to check.
