### ▸ FAQ

**Q: Is RM 6,800 not a bit much? I saw RM 1,500 on Facebook.**
Yes, and you'll get a RM 1,500 result. Those are templates with your logo dropped in, usually built offshore, usually with hosting locked to them. We write every line of copy for your clinic, structure the site around how patients actually decide, and hand you full ownership. If RM 6,800 is the wrong budget, we'd rather tell you now than waste your time.

**Q: Will this get me into trouble with MMC / MDC advertising rules?**
We build within the Malaysian Medical Council and Malaysian Dental Council guidelines — factual information, qualifications, services and fees, no outcome claims, no before-and-afters without proper handling, no "best in JB." We'll flag anything risky before it goes live. Final sign-off is yours.

**Q: How does the WhatsApp booking actually work?**
It's not a complicated system. A patient taps the button, WhatsApp opens with a message already written — "Hi, I'd like to book a scaling appointment, preferably Saturday morning." Your staff reply as normal. No new software, no new login, no training. It works on Singapore numbers too.

**Q: Three weeks — really?**
Yes, if you reply to us. Week 1: content and structure. Week 2: design and build. Week 3: your review, revisions, launch. The projects that drag are the ones where we wait two weeks for doctor photos. We'll chase you politely.

**Q: I already have a website. Can you just fix it?**
Sometimes. Send us the link on WhatsApp. If it's a decent foundation we'll tell you and quote smaller. If it's built on something we can't salvage, we'll say that too.

**Q: Who actually owns the site after?**
You do. Domain in your name, hosting in your name, all logins handed over in a document. If you never speak to us again, your site keeps running. That's the point.

**Q: Do you work with clinics outside JB?**
We're JB-based and prefer clinics we can visit — JB, Skudai, Bukit Indah, Mount Austin, Permas Jaya, Johor Jaya, Kulai, Senai, Pasir Gudang. Further afield, we can do it fully remote. Ask.

**Q: Why only 4 clinics a month?**
Because we write the copy ourselves and we don't want your site to read like the clinic down the road. When the month's full, it's full.

## QUICK DEPLOYMENT NOTES

- **Test first:** Scripts 1 (missed calls) and 3 (outdated site) — strongest pain, broadest reach. Run EN and Mandarin versions as separate ad sets, don't let the algorithm blend them.
- **Targeting:** Interest-stack *dental practice management / clinic ownership* + job titles, radius 40km around JB, ages 32–58. Layer a lookalike off anyone who taps the WhatsApp link.
- **Creative format:** Shoot vertical 9:16, hard-sub everything — most of this is watched on mute in a clinic between patients.
- **Lead handling:** Every ad points to the same `wa.me` link with a UTM-style tag per script (`?text=` prefix differs slightly) so you know which hook brought them in.
- **Hold the price line.** The entire offer rests on "fixed price, no retainer." The moment you discount it in DMs, the positioning is gone.
