How the trial works

One tap from the ad to your front desk's WhatsApp.

01The ad

Shown on Facebook and Instagram to adults living or working around The Strand, in English, 中文 and Bahasa Malaysia.

02One tap

"Send message" opens WhatsApp to 014-365 0270 with a message already typed, e.g. "Hi Smile Arts Kota Damansara, I'd like to ask about implants".

03Your reply

Your front desk answers as usual (we supply ready replies in all three languages) and books the consultation.

04We track it

Each enquiry names the treatment, so a one-line log (date, treatment, booked yes/no) shows exactly what the ads bring in.

Set-up

You approve the ads; we link WhatsApp and submit the ads to Meta.

Day 1

Ads go live.

Day 3

We pause weak ads and move budget to the ones bringing enquiries.

Day 7

Short WhatsApp update to you: enquiries so far.

Day 14

Report and a 20-minute call: enquiries, cost per enquiry, bookings, best treatment.

Your cost for the trial: none. 4Skales covers the ad spend for the 2 weeks.Nothing runs until you approve every ad.
