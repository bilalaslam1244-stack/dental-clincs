01 · The plan

English for everyone nearby, Chinese for Chinese speakers.

Why not boost every ad and language

  • 6 ads × 3 languages in 4 ad sets on a small budget is about RM 7 per ad set: none gets enough chats for Meta to learn who books.
  • Meta shows the Chinese version of an ad only to people whose app is set to Chinese. Many Malaysian Chinese use English, so adding Chinese to an ad isn't enough to reach them.
  • Malay waits for week 2 or the paid month, so the budget goes where his patients are.

What we do: 2 ad sets, 5 ads

  • Ad set 1 · Main (RM 30/day): 3 ads in English, each with a Chinese version added, shown to everyone within 8 km.
  • Ad set 2 · 中文 (RM 15/day): 2 Chinese-only ads, shown only to people whose app is in Chinese, within 10 km.
  • Budgets set per ad set, so the smaller Chinese set isn't starved by the bigger one.
  • Day 3 we compare cost per chat between the two and move money to the cheaper one.

08 · The 14 days

What happens and when.

Before launch

Dr. Daren approves the 5 ads; WhatsApp 014-365 0270 linked to the Page; front desk ready with English and 中文 quick replies and the one-line log.

Day 1

Campaign goes live. Don't edit anything for 72 hours; every edit restarts Meta's learning.

Day 3

Compare cost per chat between the two ad sets. Pause any ad with 0 chats after about RM 40 spent on it.

Day 4–8

Move RM 5–10/day to the cheaper ad set (keep at least RM 10 in the other). Day 7: WhatsApp update to Dr. Daren; swap the weakest ad.

Day 14

Report by language: chats, bookings, cost per booking, best ad. 20-minute call and monthly plan.

Next ads in line for week 2

Main: 1. Implant RM 5,000 (once the price is confirmed) 2. Veneers. 中文: Braces 中文, then Implant RM 5,000 中文. Malay versions are ready for the paid month.

If one ad set clearly wins

Raise its daily budget by at most 20% at a time, never every day. Inside each ad set, Meta already gives the winning ad more spend.

What we measure

WhatsApp conversations started from the ads, and how many became booked consultations (from the front desk's log). Bookings matter more than clicks.
