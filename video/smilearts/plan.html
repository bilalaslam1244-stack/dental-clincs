01 · Why only three ads

A small budget works best when it isn't spread thin.

What not to do: all 6 ads in 4 ad sets

  • RM 30 a day split four ways is about RM 7 per ad set, which buys only a handful of conversations a day across the whole campaign.
  • Each ad set needs its own data to learn who books. Spread this thin, none of them gets enough, and Meta keeps guessing.
  • After 3 days you'd have too few chats per ad to tell what works.

What we do: 1 ad set, 3 ads, one budget

  • One ad set around the clinic: all the budget feeds one learning pool, and Meta shifts spend to whichever ad gets the most chats.
  • Three ads covering a quick win and two of Dr. Daren's focus treatments: enough to compare, few enough to fund properly.
  • RM 40 a day (RM 30 minimum) for 14 days, about RM 560 in total.
  • On Day 3 we cut what isn't working; in week 2 we swap in a new ad.

04 · The 14 days

What happens and when.

Before launch

Dr. Daren approves the 3 ads; WhatsApp 014-365 0270 linked to the Page; front desk ready with quick replies and the one-line log.

Day 1

Campaign goes live. Don't edit anything for 72 hours; every edit restarts Meta's learning.

Day 3

Check conversations per ad. Pause any ad with 0 chats after about RM 40 spent on it. Keep the rest.

Day 7–8

WhatsApp update to Dr. Daren. Swap the weakest ad for the next one in line (see below).

Day 14

Report: chats, bookings, cost per booking, best ad. 20-minute call and monthly plan.

Next ads in line for week 2

1. Implant RM 5,000 (once Dr. Daren confirms the price). 2. Porcelain veneers. 3. Clear aligners RM 6,000 (once confirmed).

If one ad clearly wins

Leave the budget where it is and let Meta give it more spend. Raise the daily budget by at most 20% at a time, never every day.

What we measure

WhatsApp conversations started from the ads, and how many became booked consultations (from the front desk's log). Bookings matter more than clicks.
