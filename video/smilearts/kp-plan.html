04 · The 14 days

What happens and when.

Before launch

Dr. Daren approves the ads and confirms prices; WhatsApp 011-6427 5645 linked to the Kepong Page; front desk ready with replies and the one-line log.

Day 1

Ads go live. Don't edit anything for 72 hours; every edit restarts Meta's learning.

Day 3

Pause any ad with 0 chats after about RM 40. Check Frequency: fine under 2.

Day 7–8

Update to Dr. Daren. Swap the weakest ad for the backup (RM 399) or the best Kota Damansara ad.

Day 14

Report: chats, bookings, cost per booking, best ad at Kepong. Plan the paid month.

Total spend, both branches

Kota Damansara RM 60/day + Kepong RM 30/day = RM 90/day, about RM 1,260 over 14 days.

Compare like with like

Compare Kepong's ads with each other. Kepong and Kota Damansara are different areas with different people, so their numbers aren't a head-to-head test.

What we measure

WhatsApp chats from the ads and how many became booked consultations, from the Kepong front desk's one-line log.
